Evaluating language models on production data can be costly when every assessment requires fresh human labels. This coauthored study explores ensemble disagreement as a proxy for model error on keyphrase extraction across studied languages and domains. It provides evidence for a lower-cost evaluation signal, not a universal replacement for human judgment.
