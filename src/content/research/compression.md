How do you bring multilingual NLP into production without multiplying deployment overhead? Our work combines cross-lingual, multi-task modeling with distillation. The published study covers classification in 12 languages and reports estimated hardware-cost savings of 44% compared with the original full-size model. Those are study-specific estimates, not a general guarantee.
